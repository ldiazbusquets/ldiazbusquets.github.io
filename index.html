Projects

Tools I've built for investing and research.

ARGUS Portfolio Planner

A portfolio analysis tool that runs in the browser. It takes holdings lot by lot and shows how the portfolio might behave, how it would have behaved, and what is really inside each ETF.

Built with React. Imports and exports Excel.

  • Monte Carlo simulation with 1,500 paths
  • Historical backtest on 1928 to 2025 data
  • Efficient frontier and CAPM beta estimation
  • ETF look-through to underlying holdings
  • 13 tabs, with print-ready reports

LADON & HERETIC

Two research agents. LADON watches my holdings and watchlist for catalysts; HERETIC looks for new opportunities and reads the filings in depth.
