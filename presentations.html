Three group projects from my finance and business classes: BlackRock and Intuit for FIN 315, and a strategy recommendation for Hilti in BUS 400. Each deck is here slide by slide, with the full PDF to open or download.

01 FIN 315, Financial Institutions and Markets

BlackRock, and two of its funds.

A look at BlackRock as a firm (its history, scale, leadership, assets under management and financial statements), then two of its funds side by side.

For the iShares Nasdaq Premium Income Active ETF (BALQ), our verdict was a conditional watch rather than a primary position: a low 0.35% expense ratio and an active quant approach, but only months of history, a small asset base, and a first distribution that was mostly return of capital.

For the BlackRock Global Allocation Fund (MALOX), steady long-term returns at about index-level risk, with fees that can make a plain index ETF the better choice.

Team: Joel DaSilva, Luis Diaz Busquets, John Everitt, Alex Fucile, Hunter Glickel, Yoselin Gomez

02 FIN 315, Financial Institutions and Markets

Intuit as a fintech company.

Why Intuit, the company behind TurboTax, Credit Karma, QuickBooks and Mailchimp, counts as fintech: its history, value proposition, competitors, a SWOT and Porter analysis, and what it offers clients and the company itself.

It also covers the regulatory side, from FTC enforcement and a $141 million state settlement to the IRS Direct File program, and ends with a growth recommendation: acquire Databricks to strengthen AI and data, or smaller AI and fintech firms if that is not feasible.

Team: Dylan Bibeault, Luis Diaz Busquets, Satanvi Kavali, Markus Martinez, Joseph Nuzzi, Kyle Provencher

03 BUS 400, prepared for Hilti

Proving value beyond the price tag.

A strategy recommendation built around a challenge from Hilti. From the market, competitors such as Milwaukee, DeWalt, Makita and Bosch, and interviews with five construction professionals, we found that premium pricing, brand loyalty and battery lock-in make new contractors hard to win.

Our proposal is AI-supported account management for Hilti’s direct sales team, built on the ON!Track and Fieldwire tools it already has, so each sales rep can show a contractor the value of Hilti beyond the upfront price.

Team: Luke Coleman, Luis Diaz Busquets, Chase Martin, Patrick O’Keefe, Andrew Sheehan
